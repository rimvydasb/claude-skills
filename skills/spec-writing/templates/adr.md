# Architectural Decisions Record Template

File: `docs/architecture-adr.md`. This is the only document that records reasoning, rejected alternatives, and past or
removed behavior.

- **ADDED:** completely new behavior. Write `-` in the Before column.
- **REPLACED:** new behavior instead of old behavior.
- **DEPRECATED:** behavior that was removed.

Append new decisions at the end of the table; do not renumber existing rows. Each cell has at most 2 sentences. Record a
rejected alternative in the Change cell of the related decision: `Rejected: <alternative> - <reason>.`

TEMPLATE STARTS ENDS.
